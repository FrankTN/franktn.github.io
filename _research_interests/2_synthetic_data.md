With colleagues from the [ICAI Stroke Lab](https://icai-stroke-lab.webflow.io/) we are investigating potential use-cases for synthetic tabular data for prediction modelling. Specifically, we aim to mitigate class imbalance by generating synthetic samples for minority classes, thereby improving predictive performance.
