Recent work on [perfDSA](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40272658/) demonstrates the potential of fully automatic and quantitative frameworks for perfusion analysis on DSA, analagous to more established modalities such as CTP and MRP. We are currently validating perfusion DSA to assess its clinical applicability, with the goal of enabling real-time decision-making during thrombectomy.
